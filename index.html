Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

SpendSense packs a full suite of personal finance tools into a clean, focused app. No bloat, no upsells, no surprises.

Transaction Tracking
Log income and expenses with rich detail — categories, notes, dates, and wallet assignment. Every transaction, perfectly organised.
Multiple Wallets
Manage all your bank accounts, credit cards, and digital wallets in one place. Switch between them in a single tap.
Budget Management
Set monthly budgets per category. SpendSense alerts you before you overspend — so you always stay in control of your finances and hit your savings goals.

Smart Analytics
Beautiful charts and trend analysis — pie charts, bar charts, and spending breakdowns. Understand your money at a glance.
Custom Categories
Create and manage your own spending categories with custom icons. Organise money your way — not by someone else's template.

Dark & Light Mode

Privacy First

Your money data
is yours. Full stop.

Most finance apps profit from your data. SpendSense is different — we never see it, touch it, or transmit it. Period.

Completely offline
All data lives in a local SQLite database on your device. No internet connection is ever required to use the app.
Zero data collection
No analytics. No telemetry. No crash reporting. We have no servers, no user database, and no visibility into your data — whatsoever.
No account needed
Download and start using instantly. No email, no sign-up, no subscription. Just open the app and take control of your finances.
Free forever, no ads
SpendSense is completely free — no premium tier, no paywalls, no in-app purchases, and absolutely no advertisements. Ever.
